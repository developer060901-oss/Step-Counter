Privacy Policy

Application: Step Counter • Developer: Meera Devi • Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Privacy Commitment: Step Counter is designed as an offline, privacy-first fitness app. We do not sell your personal data, we do not show advertisements, and your step history is stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Step Counter ("the App"), developed and published by Meera Devi ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information related to your use of the App. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the App processes, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Step Counter is built on a local-first architecture. The App does not require user registration, email sign-ins, or account creation.

A. Fitness & Physical Activity Data

  • Step Count & Cadence: Real-time step counts detected using your device's built-in step counter hardware sensors.
  • Activity Metrics: Estimated distance covered, calories burned, and active duration calculated mathematically based on your step counts.
  • Hydration Intake: Water intake amounts manually logged by you to track your daily hydration goals.

Storage & Access: All fitness records and hydration logs are saved exclusively within a secure, encrypted on-device database (SQLite/Room). They are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

B. Personal Information

We do not collect personal identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or financial details.

3. Android Device Permissions

To deliver accurate step counting and timely reminders, the App requests only the strictly necessary system permissions:

  • Physical Activity (android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION): Required to access the on-device step sensor to record your daily steps while walking, jogging, or running.
  • Health Foreground Service (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH): Required to keep the pedometer sensor active continuously in the background when your phone screen is locked or while multitasking, ensuring no steps are missed.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to display ongoing step counts in your status bar and deliver optional daily step goal or hydration reminders.
  • Start at Boot (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Allows the background step tracking service to automatically resume after your phone is rebooted.
  • Body Sensors (android.permission.BODY_SENSORS & HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS): Utilized strictly on compatible hardware to capture precise accelerometer step intervals.

4. Advertisements & Third-Party Tracking

100% Ad-Free: Step Counter contains NO advertisements. All third-party advertising SDKs (including Google AdMob) have been completely removed.

We do not use advertising identifiers (AAID), cross-site tracking cookies, or commercial telemetry frameworks.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, we do not sell, rent, trade, or share your fitness or personal information with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • In-App Clear Data: You can reset or clear your step history and water intake records at any time within the App's Settings.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored databases, preferences, and records from your device immediately.

7. Children's Privacy

Step Counter complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Security

Your data is protected by Android's application sandbox security model, preventing other apps from accessing your private fitness database.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
